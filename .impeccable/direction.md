# Direction contract: Diwaniya Tonight v1 (all surfaces)

Mode: Operate. Source: ADC-RES-50 (UI Research row) built on ADC-RES-49. Development-only: never copy into app source or anything served.

THESIS: Tonight's open seats, read in one glance. Every table is drawn from above with its seats around it, so "one short at the Kout table" is a picture, not a sentence. Refuses the category default of a list of event cards with a seat count in small grey type, and the casino-felt card-game look.

OWN-WORLD: Night-wool ground (#15111A / #1E1824 / #2A2231), wool-white ink, madder red for actions and the active pill, saffron only for open seats and pings, camel for regulars and table rims. A 6px woven band (own repeat after Sadu's banded structure) under every app bar, on sheet grabbers and on Welcome. Readex Pro alone, 400/500/700, tabular figures, Arabic first and right to left.

STORY: A guest sees which diwaniya near him is one seat short tonight, asks for that seat, and gets the pin only once the host says yes. A host opens the night, watches seats fill, calls for one more, and the result of the game lands in the league everyone keeps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tonight. App bar with title, woven band, filter chips (game, area, time, audience) in one scrolling row, then diwaniya cards each led by its seat rings (64-112px) and the saffron "N seats open" line; the bottom bar holds 5 labelled tabs with the madder pill under Tonight. Primary action on a card: the card itself opens S6, where "Request a seat" sits above the bar.

FORM: Seat ring per table (choice 1 of the ordered list in ADC-RES-50 section 1). seed: none (unavailable) — the concept-seed launcher is not installed under ADC manual mode.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
